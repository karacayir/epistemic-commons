# Europe & Treaties

Forty-eight years of constitutional history in three statutes: the Act that made
European law flow into the United Kingdom (1972), the Act that cut the conduit (2018),
and the Act that settled the terms of departure (2020). No other codex shows so
clearly why history must be preserved rather than overwritten — half a century of law
was made under the 1972 Act, and understanding today's "assimilated law" requires the
full chain of inscriptions.

## Documents in this codex

| Document | Citation | Status |

## Why these live together

They are one story: accession, incorporation, referendum, withdrawal, and the
management of the residue. Each Act expressly amends or repeals its predecessor, and
the referendum inscriptions of 1975 and 2016 bracket the deepest constitutional
argument in modern British history.
