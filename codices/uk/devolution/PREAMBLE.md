# Devolution Settlements

In 1998 the United Kingdom performed the largest redistribution of legislative power in
its modern history: three statutes created (or re-created) legislatures in Edinburgh,
Cardiff and Belfast, each authorised by referendum. In this platform's vocabulary,
devolution is the **fork**: a polity derives new institutions from the parent codex
while remaining within the Union's statute branch.

## Why these live together

Three parallel settlements, drafted in the same two years, amended in visible dialogue
with one another (reserved-powers models, permanence clauses, consent conventions), and
each carrying a referendum inscription — the people's assent recorded before the
chambers'. They are the template for how this platform models multi-level polities.
